## 🛠️ Technical Questions

### Q: Why Golang over Node.js for backend?
**A:** Golang's compiled nature, strong concurrency model (goroutines), and explicit error handling make it a natural fit for high-throughput microservices. At RNR Consulting, switching to Golang + gRPC gave us a 35% improvement in inter-service communication efficiency over REST.

### Q: Why gRPC over REST for microservices?
**A:** For inter-service communication, gRPC gives strongly-typed contracts (Protocol Buffers), bi-directional streaming, and 30% less serialization overhead compared to REST+JSON. REST is still the right choice for external-facing APIs where broad client compatibility matters.

### Q: What's your experience with event-driven architectures?
**A:** I've worked with NATS JetStream in production — managing event-driven workflows across 6+ services. The key insight is that full producer-consumer decoupling improves fault tolerance significantly, especially when services need to scale independently.

### Q: How do you approach API security?
**A:** At a gateway level: JWT authentication, rate limiting, and custom plugin-based routing via Kong API Gateway — this is what we use at RNR to secure 100% of externally exposed endpoints. At the service level: input validation, parameterized queries, and proper error handling to avoid leaking system internals.
